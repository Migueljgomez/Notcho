# Today

Finish by the end of today.

- **Horizon:** today only, from now until the end of their working hours.
- **Tasks:** 15 to 60 minutes each. Fewer, smaller tasks beat a long list.
- **Fit:** free time is what is left of today. If the work is more than about 80% of it, say plainly what has to move to tomorrow and ask which.
- **Blocks:** block every task, back to back in the free gaps, starting at the next half hour.
- **Plan page:** the handoff only. Under "Next", name what rolls to tomorrow.
